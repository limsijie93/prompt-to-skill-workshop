# Lesson 02 · Brief to Backlog (5 minutes)

**Learning objective:** use a reusable skill to turn a project brief into Jira-ready epics and tickets, with estimates your team can challenge.

**Learners:** project leads and coordinators · **Tool:** Claude Cowork

| Phase | What happens | Material |
|---|---|---|
| I do | The skill's four parts, then a brief becomes epics, stories, three-point estimates, a critical path, top risks and a Jira CSV, with no instructions typed | [`breaking-down-projects-for-jira`](skills/breaking-down-projects-for-jira/SKILL.md), [sample brief](data/project-brief-ai-for-managers.md) |
| We do | [Fix the ticket](activities/fix-the-ticket.md): learners name what a one-line prompt's ticket is missing, and see their fixes are the skill's Checks | activity |
| You do | Add your team's Definition of Ready to the skill and run your next brief through it | [templates](../../templates) |

Estimates are ranges with their key assumption, labelled a draft for team review. Roadmaps and status reports are the next skills in the same chain.

**Facilitator:** [run-sheet.md](run-sheet.md), which also explains what was cut to fit 5 minutes · **Upload-ready skill:** [`dist/`](dist)
