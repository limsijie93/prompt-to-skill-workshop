# Stakeholder ask for the Part 1 live demo

Fictional, for training use only. Paste this into the Cowork task with `roadmap-ai-programmes.md` attached.

> Here's our programme roadmap. A pilot client's Head of L&D asked: "Can we add a module on responsible AI for managers to the pilot?" Does it fit?

## What good output looks like

- **Roadmap in brief:** goal, O1–O3 with deadlines, E1 in progress (owner: instructional designer).
- **Gaps, quoting the roadmap:** E1's scope includes "course outline and learning outcomes", but "new course topics" are not on this roadmap. The roadmap doesn't say how scope changes to the pilot are approved, or whether the 1-day agenda has room.
- **Decision:** Unclear, ask first (confidence Medium or Low). It would become Fits now if it's a section inside the existing 1-day outline, or Doesn't fit if it's an extra module or new course.
- **Questions to the requester:** Is this a section within the 1-day workshop, or extra time? Is it needed for the 15 Jan pilot, or for the wider rollout? What's driving it (for example, a policy or compliance need)?
- **Questions to the roadmap owner (product manager):** If it goes into the day, what comes out? Can E1 absorb it before the 4 Dec content deadline and SME reviews?
- **Draft reply:** thanks the client, says you're checking fit with the product team, and gives a date to reply by; it doesn't promise the module.
