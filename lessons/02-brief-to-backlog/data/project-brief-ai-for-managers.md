# Project brief – "AI for People Managers" pilot

Fictional, for training use only.

**Goal:** launch a new 1-day corporate workshop, "AI for People Managers", and run a pilot with 2 corporate clients.
**Deadline:** pilot class delivered by **15 Jan 2027**. Kickoff: 5 Oct 2026.

**Team**
- Product manager (owner)
- Instructional designer
- Associate trainer (external, available from 1 Dec)
- Ops coordinator
- LMS admin (part-time, 2 days a week)

**Scope**
- Course outline and learning outcomes
- Slides and facilitator guide
- 3 hands-on labs using AI tools
- Pre-course survey for learners
- Course listing on the LMS
- Trainer onboarding and a dry run
- Pilot delivery and a feedback review

**Constraints**
- Subject-matter expert (SME) review takes about 1 week each round; 2 rounds expected.
- LMS listing needs 10 working days for approval after submission.
- Office closed 24 Dec 2026 – 1 Jan 2027.
- Pilot clients need confirmed dates at least 3 weeks before the class.
