# Roadmap – AI for People Managers programme (Q4 2026 – Q2 2027)

Fictional, for training use only.

**Context:** you are the Technical Program Manager (TPM) on a learning-content team at a training provider. The team builds courses that help working professionals make the transition into AI. This roadmap is for one of those programmes: **AI for People Managers**, for managers who need to lead teams that now work with AI tools.

**Goal:** launch a 1-day corporate workshop, "AI for People Managers", prove it with a pilot, then scale it.

| Phase | Outcome (and deadline) | Epics |
|---|---|---|
| **Q4 2026** | **O1 · Pilot delivered to 2 corporate clients by 15 Jan 2027** | E1 Pilot course content ready · E2 LMS listing live · E3 Trainer ready · E4 Pilot delivered and reviewed |
| **Q1 2027** | O2 · Running with 5 clients by 31 Mar 2027 | E5 Course revised from pilot feedback · E6 Mandarin version for regional clients · E7 Sales enablement kit |
| **Q2 2027** | O3 · Self-paced version live by 30 Jun 2027 | E8 E-learning build (vendor) · E9 LMS completion reporting |

**Not on this roadmap:** custom work for a single client (e.g. bespoke dashboards), new course topics.

## Epic in focus: E1 · Pilot course content ready

- **Serves:** O1, pilot delivered by 15 Jan 2027
- **Due:** content final by **4 Dec 2026**, so the trainer (available from 1 Dec) can onboard and run a dry run before the holidays
- **Scope:** course outline and learning outcomes; slides; facilitator guide; 3 hands-on labs using AI tools; pre-course learner survey
- **Out of scope:** extra labs, translations, client-specific versions

## Team

- Product manager (roadmap owner)
- Instructional designer (E1 owner)
- Associate trainer (external, available from 1 Dec)
- Ops coordinator
- LMS admin (part-time, 2 days a week)

## Constraints

- Kickoff 5 Oct 2026.
- Subject-matter expert (SME) review takes about 1 week per round; 2 rounds expected.
- LMS listing needs 10 working days for approval after submission.
- Office closed 24 Dec 2026 – 1 Jan 2027.
- Pilot clients need confirmed dates at least 3 weeks before the class.
